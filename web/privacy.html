Privacy and your data

Last updated 16 November 2026

RTC Rentals is run by Ryno-Tec Consulting (Pty) Ltd (registration 2026/818634/07), South Africa. This page explains what the app keeps about you, why, and how to have it removed, under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Who uses the app

  • Owners (landlords) manage their properties, tenants, rent, leases, inspections and maintenance.
  • Tenants are invited by their owner to see their lease, rent, receipts and repairs.
  • Maintenance people are invited by an owner to see the jobs assigned to them.
  • Witnesses sign a lease through a one-time link, without an account.

For the details an owner captures about their tenants and team, the owner is the responsible party and Ryno-Tec processes the information for them. For account details, Ryno-Tec is the responsible party.

What we keep

  • Account: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), and your role.
  • Rental records: property and unit details, lease terms, rent charged and paid, receipts.
  • Tenant details the owner enters: name, contact details and ID number for the lease.
  • Leases you sign: the text, your name, typed and drawn signature, time, network address and browser, so the signature can be proven (Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002).
  • Inspections and repairs: notes, ratings and photos of the home.
  • Messages between tenant and owner, and job updates from maintenance people.

We do not sell your information, show adverts, or track you across other apps or websites. The app has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Why we use it

Only to run the rental: to manage the lease, record rent, keep the condition of the home on record, fix faults, and keep the records the law requires (for example for SARS). An owner sees the information about their own properties, tenants and team only. A tenant sees only their own lease and home. A maintenance person sees only the jobs assigned to them, with the address and the tenant’s name and number so they can arrange access.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in) in a data centre in the European Union, which has data-protection law at least as strong as POPIA. Photos and signed documents are kept in private storage that needs a signed-in account to open. All connections are encrypted.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. Rental and payment records are kept for five years after a lease ends, because tax law requires it. Signed leases and confirmed inspections are kept for as long as either party may need them as evidence, normally three years after the lease ends.

Your rights, and deleting your account

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. To delete your account, email rentals@ryno-tec.co.za from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my RTC Rentals account”. We confirm within 7 days and delete your account and personal information within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep (rent and payment records, signed leases), which are kept only for that purpose and then deleted.

A tenant’s or maintenance person’s records also belong to the owner’s rental records; deleting your account removes your login and personal details, while the owner keeps what the law requires them to keep.

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.
